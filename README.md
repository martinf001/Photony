# Photony Theory

Photony Theory is a proposed physical framework developed by **Dr. Fred Lane Martin** to explore the underlying structure of space, energy, matter, electromagnetism, gravitation, and time.

The theory proposes that familiar electromagnetic and particle phenomena may arise from organized structures involving **Planck Space, energy filaments, elemental charge photons, electron-mediated transduction, electric chains, magnetic chains, and classical photons**.

Photony does not replace established physics. Maxwell's equations, electromagnetic theory, quantum mechanics, relativity, plasma physics, and experimental measurement remain essential constraints. Photony asks a complementary question:

**What physical organization might underlie the quantities and behaviors that established theories describe so successfully?**

## About Dr. Fred Lane Martin

Fred Lane Martin, Ph.D., is an engineer, researcher, inventor, author, educator, and registered USPTO patent agent. He earned his Ph.D. in Energy Science and Engineering through the Bredesen Center at the University of Tennessee in 2026.

His work spans electrical engineering, energy science, electromagnetic systems, solar and plasma phenomena, intellectual property, and fundamental physical theory. He is the author of *Photony Foundations* and multiple volumes of *The Photony Compendium*.
